Production Engineering
Laboratory Reports

Nine complete write-ups covering every section of the Production Engineering Laboratory and Workshop Practice 301 & 302 manual — health and safety, engineering measurement, woodwork, sheet metal, bench fitting, welding, machine shop, automotive systems and foundry practice.

Each report follows the standard laboratory format: aims, objectives, apparatus, theory and diagrams, procedure, precautions, discussion and conclusion, together with worked answers to the questions set in the manual.

HSE 301

Health and Safety Management in Workplaces

The three-step approach to workplace safety, statutory duties of workplace partners, ergonomics of manual handling, optimal working heights and the general shop safety rules that govern every other experiment in this manual.

Read the report → EM 301

Study of Workshop Measuring Devices

Line-standard and face-standard angular measurement: protractors, universal bevel protractors, sine bars, angle gauges, spirit levels and clinometers, including the derivation that limits sine-bar use to 45°.

Read the report → WW 301

Woodwork Practice

Timber classification and conversion, the six families of carpentry hand tools, and the production of two framing joints — the bridle joint and the mortise and tenon joint — from 300 × 60 × 42 mm stock.

Read the report → SM 301

Sheet Metal Work

Surface development by the parallel-line and radial-line methods, hems, seams and wired edges, and the fabrication of a hexagonal prism and a frustum-of-cone funnel from 28-gauge galvanised plain sheet.

Read the report → BF 301

Fitting Shop Practice — Taps and Dies

Internal and external thread generation by hand: taper, plug and bottoming taps, split and collet dies, tapping drill selection, the half-turn-back rule, and the production of a matching bolt and nut.

Read the report → HS 301

Welding Practice — Oxy-Acetylene

Gas welding equipment and gas pressures, the three oxy-acetylene flame settings and what each is good for, filler rods and fluxes, resistance welding, and the joining of two mild-steel pieces by gas welding.

Read the report → MS 301

Thread Cutting and Knurling on the Lathe

Screw cutting with a single-point tool: the spindle-to-lead-screw ratio, quick-change gearboxes, the thread-chasing dial, depth-of-cut calculation, and a full turning sequence on a ø25 mm mild steel bar.

Read the report → AS 301 & 302

Vehicle Systems

The four integrated systems of a motor vehicle — ignition, fuel, cooling and transmission — their components, their common faults and the remedies, plus firing order and firing interval for four- and six-cylinder engines.

Read the report → FS 301

Foundry Practice — Sand Moulding

Patterns and pattern allowances, moulding sand constituents and the eight properties that matter, foundry hand tools, and the preparation of green-sand moulds from a single-piece pattern and a split pattern.

Read the report →
